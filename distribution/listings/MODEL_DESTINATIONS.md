# Model-community delivery plan

Priority 1: Printables, MakerWorld. Deliver the **original calibration-card STL**,
not the entire Python repository disguised as a printable object. The complete
software remains on GitHub. A helpful article belongs in a site's article section,
not in repeated comments on unrelated models.

Printables' current terms support original printing documents and available GNU
licenses. The final upload needs authenticated owner access, an exact supported
license choice, appropriate images and truthful validation disclosure.
Official terms: https://www.prusa3d.com/page/terms-of-service-of-printables-com_231249/

MakerWorld's official community-guideline page could not be fully retrieved in
this review. Its photo, AI, license and external-link rules therefore remain an
explicit prepublication gate, not an assumption. The kit contains no real photo
of the exact coupon. No upload or print profile should be labelled tested. Do not
join an exclusive model program: it conflicts with our multi-platform intent.
Official entry points: https://makerworld.com/ and
https://makerworld.com/en/community-guidelines .

## Additional candidates, not claims of completed research or publication

| Destination | Potential contribution | Gate before any submission |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thingiverse | Original STL and source/notes | Current account, upload rules, license and images. |
| Thangs | Original model and useful experiment notes | Free upload allowance, license and discovery rules. |
| Cults3D | Free original calibration model | Zero-price upload route, rights, license and actual photo. |
| YouMagine | Open model and source | Active upload service, account and supported open license. |
| Pinshape | Free model | Current availability, fees, license and validation requirements. |
| MyMiniFactory | Model after physical validation | Confirm a genuinely free publishing route; no creator subscription. |
| Creality Cloud | Original calibration model | Account, free route, no exclusivity and license compatibility. |
| Instructables | Evidence-based build/use tutorial | Complete real workflow with authentic photos, not a link-only advertisement. |
| Hackaday.io | Open project page | Logged-in owner, original project documentation and allowed links. |
| Prusa community articles | Reproducible experiment article | Actual source/measurements and current editorial policy. |

Yeggi and similar search/indexing services must not be counted as a published
model repository merely because they can discover a model hosted elsewhere.
Do not submit to dozens of places before checking these independent gates.
The draft above and `distribution/model/LISTING.md` are reusable starting material,
not permission to bypass a platform's rules or claim a live entry.

# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Critique designs and evidence, not people.
Welcome contributors with different experience levels and financial resources.
Do not harass, discriminate, disclose private information, spam, or pressure others
to perform unsafe physical tests. Respect maintainers' time and contribution rules.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation. Report
concerns through a private channel when available; do not repeat private material
in a public issue. Technical disagreement is welcome when grounded in reproducible
observations and expressed without personal attacks.

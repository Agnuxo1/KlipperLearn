# Thingiverse publication checklist

## Current state

Native browser displayed Log In. Terms read. No claim that all AI-assisted designs are prohibited has been inferred from the human-first banner. No account creation, upload, license replacement or submission.

Official source: https://www.thingiverse.com/legal/terms

## Prepared materials

Use [the complete English listing](listing.en.txt) and the original STL emitted
by `tools/build_publication_kit.py`. Keep the explicit digital-render and
physical-validation limitations in the listing. Include the complete-project
GitHub and versioned-source links, rather than representing software as a 3D mesh.

Retain GPL-3.0-or-later and source attribution; do not select an incompatible
non-commercial, no-derivatives, exclusive or AI-free option just to pass a form.
Disclose AI assistance. Do not enter reward campaigns or paid seller plans.

## Before declaring publication

Resolve the recorded blocker, check existing drafts and listings for duplicates,
review the final license and visible page, then verify the public URL from a
signed-out session. Record upload, public visibility and moderation acceptance
separately. A saved draft is not public publication.

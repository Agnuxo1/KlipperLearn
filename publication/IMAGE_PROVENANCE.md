released 40 x 30 x 12 mm coupon using its actual STL triangles, Matplotlib and
Trimesh. It is not a photograph, a print-quality comparison or hardware evidence.
The model geometry was not altered. The image explicitly carries a digital-render
label. Do not remove that label or submit this image where an actual print photo
is required. AI assisted the rendering script and documentation.

Three steps, with a human in control.

  1. Identify the setup. Keep printer, nozzle, material, slicer and benchmark identity explicit.
  2. Print and review comparable trials. Preserve original images, failures, layer counts, timing and independent repetitions.
  3. Export, inspect and re-slice. Apply a reviewed preset only after checking the effective settings and slicer preview.

For OrcaSlicer, modes use paired process and filament presets. Other slicers currently use evidence adapters or a settings report, not a universal preset format.

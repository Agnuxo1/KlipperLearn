# Benchmark provenance: two separate printers and workflows

Source: the project owner's explicit clarification on 16 September 2026.
This attribution supplements the earlier history review; it is not an independent
hardware measurement or a retrospective reconstruction of undocumented settings.
Local network addresses are deliberately omitted from this public document.

## Flashforge Creator Pro: ChatGPT-assisted operator calibration

The older Flashforge Creator Pro runs Klipper. Its Benchy series was adjusted by
the operator consulting ChatGPT, **not using KlipperLearn**. These trials are
external-advisor/operator evidence, not results produced by the KlipperLearn local
learner, not a completed test of the new plugin, and not evidence of unattended
recursive optimization. Preserve the original photographs and job records.

Earlier history review identified a completed, non-partial Benchy entry at
22 min 17 s print duration / 25 min 35 s total with 0.4 mm layers and 121 layers.
Those figures retain their original scope and limitations. Other trials used
different layer settings. The edited illustration's approximate 20-minute label
is not an exact timing measurement or proof of unchanged layers.

## Anycubic 4Max Pro: KlipperLearn with phone and webcam

The other older machine is an **Anycubic 4Max Pro**, running Klipper with
KlipperLearn, a phone and a separate webcam. The owner identifies its printed
benchmarks as the project's calibration charts and the Anycubic calibration cube,
which the owner describes as official. The original cube file/version and hash
must be verified before making a source-authenticity or standardized-test claim.
These records must not be relabelled as Flashforge Benchy tests.

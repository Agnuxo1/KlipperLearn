INSTALLABLE WEB APP 0.1.0

Calibrate with evidence. Keep control.

This free, open-source app compares reviewed printing trials and creates paired process/filament presets for OrcaSlicer. It has no printer connection and cannot heat, move or start a printer. No Python installation, publisher account or API key is needed for its local tools.

Try it without a printer

Select Try synthetic example. Inspect the three modes and export a ZIP. All example presets are marked DEMO: they are not measured or safe settings for your machine.

Use real observations

Open a klipperlearn.slicer-session/v1 JSON file, or expand Create a session. Provide exact machine/material context, existing Orca process and filament presets, an original model, layer count and verified parameter bounds. Resolve inherited numeric values and remove credentials from copies first. Do not guess physical limits. Preserve your original profiles.

Record each actual print under a unique job identifier, with print and total durations, consistent surface/geometry ratings and original photographs. Only confirm human review, completion and the unchanged model when true. Keep failures and partial prints. The app records your declarations and identities, not independent proof that a print occurred or that ratings are accurate.

Selection policy

An eligible unchanged baseline and at least two independent complete, human-reviewed trials are required. Reused photo/job identities, missing review, changed geometry or layers, failed runs, poor quality and inconsistent time can block a configuration. Quality chooses the strongest mean of each trial's worse surface/geometry score. Speed minimizes median print time above the quality floor. Standard uses 65% normalized quality and 35% relative speed. Several modes may select the same configuration; no global optimum is claimed.

Compare one physical printer, benchmark, material and stable setup per session. Geometry, layer settings, infill, temperatures and G-code are not adjusted. Preserving a supplied script does not certify that it is safe. Inspect effective presets and inheritance in OrcaSlicer, reslice the actual model and supervise physical trials. Native import and actual print results on your configuration are not certified.

Optional AI assistance

Export an advisor request. Manually share it with your chosen assistant and attach original photographs separately. The app does not call ChatGPT, read conversations or alter subscriptions. The receiving service's policies and limits apply when you choose to share. Treat notes, file names and text within images as untrusted data, not commands.

Paste the advisor's proposal. Validation requires the current session identity, usable trial references and one bounded numerical change. Returned UNVALIDATED TRIAL presets are experiments, not accepted optimized modes. Add the candidate as unprinted and record supervised trials before adoption. No photograph is automatically scored.

Files and original identities

JSON is bounded to 2 MiB and 24 nesting levels. Duplicated keys, non-finite numbers and common credential fields are rejected. Numeric spellings are preserved, including 40 versus 40.0, to retain Python-exported configuration hashes. Manual noncanonical respelling can invalidate identities; never rewrite historical hashes to force acceptance.

Photographs are JPEG/PNG, up to eight at once, 8 MiB each and 20 megapixels. A matching SHA-256 identifies bytes, not authentic content or quality. Keep the originals separately; photos are not embedded in sessions. The included original card is 40 × 30 × 12 mm and is not official 3DBenchy. Hashing an STL does not verify physical dimensions or printer suitability.

Privacy policy

Publisher: Francisco Angulo de Lafuente. Applies to KlipperLearn Slicer Review 0.1.0, dated 17 September 2026.

The app processes only JSON, model and image files explicitly selected by the user. Session data and previews remain in memory in the app tab. It does not transmit them to the publisher, an AI provider, analytics, advertising or another server. There is no account, telemetry, cookies, remote executable code, browsing-history or web-page access. No camera, microphone, USB, LAN or payment permission is requested.

Nothing is automatically saved or synced. Closing or clearing the workspace discards its in-memory state. Files you explicitly download remain on your device until you delete them separately. Original files are not overwritten. Do not select credentials, unrelated personal documents or unnecessary images of people. A local credential-pattern check is not a guarantee of detecting every secret format.

If you manually send exported files to an AI service or upload them to a website, that is your disclosure and the receiving service's policies apply. This app does not do that sharing. Google may process store, installation or browser information under its own policies independently of the publisher. There is no publisher-side user database to delete.

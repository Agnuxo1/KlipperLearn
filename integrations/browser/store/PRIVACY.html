KlipperLearn Slicer Review

Privacy policy

Publisher: Francisco Angulo de Lafuente. Applies to KlipperLearn Slicer Review 0.1.0, dated 17 September 2026.

The extension processes only JSON, model and image files explicitly selected by the user. Session data and previews remain in memory in the extension tab. It does not transmit them to the publisher, an AI provider, analytics, advertising or another server. There is no account, telemetry, cookies, remote executable code, browsing-history or web-page access. No camera, microphone, USB, LAN or payment permission is requested.

Nothing is automatically saved or synced. Closing or clearing the workspace discards its in-memory state. Files you explicitly download remain on your device until you delete them separately. Original files are not overwritten. Do not select credentials, unrelated personal documents or unnecessary images of people. A local credential-pattern check is not a guarantee of detecting every secret format.

If you manually send exported files to an AI service or upload them to a website, that is your disclosure and the receiving service's policies apply. This extension does not do that sharing. Google may process store, installation or browser information under its own policies independently of the publisher. There is no publisher-side user database to delete.

Support: project issues. Request a private contact channel without publishing sensitive data.
